# Scene 1: Home (Yelahanka)

**Job of this scene:** teach the controls, establish Gudle's voice, plant the fair-play clues, introduce the pigeons. About 5 minutes. No fail state.

## Pigeon backstory (author's note)
Roodle feeds the pigeons every morning, so they wait on the balcony for her. Gudle has noticed. The pigeons being hungry and waiting is the first real clue that Roodle has been gone since last night.

## Goal
Gudle needs her car keys to leave. They are on the balcony rail, under a pigeon.

## Screens
Room (wide, pannable): bedroom with Roodle's side, kitchen corner, balcony door, front door.

## Objects and close-ups
| Object | Close-up / action | What it teaches | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone (Gudle's, always in inventory) | Call Roodle, goes to voicemail with Roodle's dry message | UI, Gudle's voice | Journal: "Unreachable" |
| Roodle's bed | Close-up of the perfectly made bed | Click to examine | Gudle: "She MADE the bed?!" Journal: "Bed made, planned ahead" |
| Charger by the bed | Close-up of charger, plugged in, no phone | Observe | Journal: "Charger left at home" (the fair-play clue) |
| Jacket on the wall | Drag it aside | Dragging moves things | Reveals half-hidden Freddy Begins poster. Gudle ignores it. Journal gets a poster photo. |
| Kitchen shelf | Take the bird seed jar | Pick up items | Seeds go into the inventory |
| Balcony door | Opens to the balcony | Moving between areas | Two pigeons on the rail, sitting on the keys |
| Pigeons (waiting, hungry) | Look first: Gudle notices they wait for Roodle. Journal: "Pigeons waiting, Roodle didn't feed them." Then use seeds (feed) OR click them while Gudle is panicked (throw something) | Items on hotspots, mood system | Either way they hop off and the keys are free. Different lines for each. |
| Keys | Pick up | | Unlocks the front door |
| Front door | Use keys | Exit | Goes to traffic scene 1 |

## Optional comic clicks (no progress, just voice)
- Check the wardrobe: empty hangers, Gudle accuses the pigeons.
- Call friends: three quick overreactions.

## Hint system
If the player idles about 30 seconds, Gudle mutters a nudge ("Where are my keys... why is it always the pigeons?").

## Chain
Seed jar (kitchen) -> pigeons on the balcony (feed or shoo) -> keys -> front door.

## Assets
Bedroom, kitchen corner, balcony, front door backgrounds. Props: bed close-up, charger, jacket, poster, seed jar, keys, phone, door. Two pigeons with three reaction poses. Gudle in a home outfit with panicked, dramatic and determined poses.
