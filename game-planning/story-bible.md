# Where's Roodle? — Story Bible (locked decisions so far)

## Premise
Roodle's friends' band, **Freddy Begins**, plays a gig at a festival on a flat ground off **NICE Road**. Roodle typed a message to Gudle ("Gone to the gig, back tmrw. Don't panic!!") and never hit send. Her phone died, her charger is still at home. Gudle, older and protective, spends the day assuming the worst.

## Locked choices
- Length: about 1 hour of play
- Gudle: older sister, protective. Her anger is concern. In the finale she is angry-crying.
- Roodle: ditzy, easygoing. When relaxed she smiles and looks up at the sky.
- Gudle realises: Roodle is fine on her own (shown, not said).
- Ending tone: mostly comic, soft at the end.
- Rant covers: the day she lost, Roodle never answers her phone, worry.

## Characters (supporting)
- **Freddy Begins**: the band. Drummer's name still to decide.
- **Mira**: artist, the drummer's girlfriend, Roodle's friend. Appears at the Gallery (as an artist) and at the festival.

## Route (and where traffic scenes go)
The Gallery, Flea Market and Cafe are all in one area, so they share ONE wide panning street (the "MAP street"). The Book House is a short hop away.

1. Home (Yelahanka)
2. Traffic scene 1: long drive south from Yelahanka
3. Police station (High Grounds, Millers Road)
4. Blossom Book House
5. Short traffic scene to the MAP street
6. MAP street, panning hub: Gallery (MAP), Flea Market (vendors out front), Cafe. Order: Gallery and Market in any order, Cafe's matching puzzle needs the market items.
7. Traffic scene 3: final drive across the city to NICE Road
8. Festival

## Clue chain
1. Home: made bed, charger still plugged in, half-hidden Freddy Begins poster
2. Police station: nothing useful, pigeon drops Blossom book
3. Book House: put the book back in its gap, find the receipt (unlocks Gallery + Market)
4. Gallery: match Roodle's handwriting in the guestbook, meet Mira's work
5. Flea Market: pick Roodle's items (sunscreen, scarf, snacks, rain layer), power bank she did not buy
6. Cafe: find the festival flyer, match it to the items
7. Traffic scenes: Freddy Begins hoarding, bus ad, passing hints
8. Festival: reunion and the unsent draft

## Finale beats
1. Gudle storms in, angry-crying, rant (lost day, never answers, worry). Friends scatter (mirrors the police station).
2. Roodle looks at her dead phone, surprised. Smiles, looks up at the sky.
3. Someone charges it. The unsent draft appears: "Gone to the gig, back tmrw. Don't panic!!"
4. Gudle sees the friends, the band, Mira. "...You have people here."
5. Hug, chocolate shake, the pigeons.
