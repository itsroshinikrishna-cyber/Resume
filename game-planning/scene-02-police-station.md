# Scene 2: Police Station (High Grounds, Millers Road)

**Job of this scene:** a comic dead end. The puzzle is real, but it leads nowhere. The only progress comes from the pigeons, and it is mostly coincidence, not a plan. About 5 minutes, no fail state.

## Author's decisions
- The officers are useless and have no information, even after the puzzle is done.
- The clue comes only because the pigeons intervene and point to an officer who happens to be heading to Blossom Book House, the same place Roodle went. Coincidence, not a plan.
- The old "pigeon drops Roodle's book" beat is replaced by this. (Knock-on change for Scene 4, see below.)

## Flow
1. Gudle walks in, officers recognise her and scatter (the "scatter" animation is reused in the finale).
2. Puzzle: find each hiding officer using tells in the room. Every officer found gives a useless result.
3. Last officer: the desk sergeant. Another useless result. Gudle admits out loud she's on her own.
4. She steps outside. The pigeons intervene and point to the one officer who is leaving. He is heading to Blossom Book House.
5. She follows him there.

## The puzzle: spot the hiding officers
| Officer | Tell (what the player notices) | Useless result |
|---|---|---|
| Behind the curtain | Boots showing under the hem | Hands her a form, "Form 27-B, fill in triplicate" |
| Under the desk | Chair still spinning | Gives her a missing-cat flyer from 2019 |
| In the filing cabinet | Drawer open a crack, cap sticking out | "Not our jurisdiction" |
| "On lunch" | Plate still steaming on the desk | "Come back tomorrow" |
| Desk sergeant | Sitting right there pretending to read | The form needs a stamp from a different office, in another building |

Journal gets one joke entry per officer, each crossed out by Gudle. No real clue is added.

Optional flavour: a "Gudle is getting louder" meter that fills if she stalls. No fail state.

## The pigeon intervention
- After the sergeant, Gudle says out loud she's on her own.
- Outside, the same two pigeons are on a lamppost. They fly down and fixate on one officer walking out, with a Blossom Book House paper bag under his arm. They hover, one lands on the bag.
- That officer did not scatter because he is new / wearing earbuds. He is leaving for lunch.
- Gudle clicks him: he's heading to Blossom Book House on MG Road to buy a gift. Roodle's favourite shop. Gudle follows.

Pigeon mood rule: if the player threw things earlier, the pigeons approach slower. Gudle always ends up feeding them: "...fine."

## Hint system
If the player idles about 30 seconds, Gudle mutters a nudge ("Boots. Whose boots are those?").

## Assets
Station interior (curtain, desk, filing cabinet, lunch plate), 5 officer sprites (4 hiding poses + 1 walking out with earbuds and bag), scatter animation, form and cat-flyer props, outside lamppost with pigeons, Blossom paper bag.
